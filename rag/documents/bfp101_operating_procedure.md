# Boiler Feed Pump 101 Operating Procedure

## High Discharge Pressure
For persistent high discharge pressure, verify pressure indication against a local gauge and confirm downstream valve alignment. Check pump operating point, suction conditions, minimum-flow/recirculation path and downstream restrictions. If pressure remains outside approved operating limits, follow the site shutdown and escalation procedure.

## Low Suction Pressure
Verify suction source availability, inlet strainer condition, valve alignment and suction instrumentation before changing operating conditions.

## Safety and Escalation
Do not bypass trips or interlocks. Escalate persistent critical alarms according to site procedure.
